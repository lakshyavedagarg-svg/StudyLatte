OUR STORY

Study + Latte = StudyLatte

StudyLatte is a student-focused reading and productivity café concept. We imagined a place where students could enjoy a comfortable café atmosphere while choosing the environment that best fits what they need to do.

Whether you're working independently, reading for pleasure, building a group project or hosting a discussion, there's a space designed for that purpose.

☕✦▤

OUR PURPOSE

One café. Different ways to learn.

Students do not always need the same kind of workspace. Quiet study calls for fewer distractions; reading benefits from comfort; teamwork needs room to talk; meetings need privacy. StudyLatte brings these needs together without treating every visit like a restaurant order.
