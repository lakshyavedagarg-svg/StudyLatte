# StudyLatte menu imagery

All menu photographs were cropped from AI-generated concept images supplied by the project group. They are illustrative images, not photographs of a real StudyLatte café. No external image hotlinks are used.

Known visual mismatches: `chocolate-cookie.png` depicts a muffin and `fruit-cup.png` depicts iced tea because the supplied images did not contain photographs of those items. Their alt text describes the pictured subject. `classic-latte.png` reuses the supplied cappuccino photograph. These substitutions should be replaced with matching group-approved images before a public commercial launch.

The original full contact sheets are not required by the website and have been excluded from this distribution to reduce its size. The individual cropped images remain unchanged.
